01 / THE IDEA

3D creativity deserves
a 3D workspace.

Generative models can turn an image or a prompt into a 3D asset. Yet shaping that result often means working through a flat screen: mapping a 2D cursor to a 3D surface, switching views to judge depth, and rotating the camera to reach hidden geometry. Expressing a spatial idea becomes a sequence of indirect controls.

VR-Crafter explores a more direct creative loop. With stereoscopic viewing and tracked controllers, artists can inspect an asset from different angles and place edits in the space it occupies. We aim to connect image-based generation, immersive AR/VR editing, and regeneration in one iterative workflow.

01

See the whole shape

Use depth cues and changing viewpoints to inspect proportion, volume, and occluded details.

02

Make spatial edits

Use tracked controllers to indicate where a change belongs directly around the 3D asset.

03

Keep the idea evolving

Feed structural changes back into generation, inspect the new result, and continue refining.

Our research direction: investigate how immersive interaction can support control and expression in generative 3D workflows.

03 / THE CREATIVE LOOP

From an image.
Into your hands.

EARLY PROTOTYPE

Our initial implementation explores a simple loop: generate a 3D model from an image, modify it through AR/VR interaction, then generate a new model from the revised structure.

THE WORKFLOW

    Create an initial model to work with.

  3. 03 / INTERACT

  4. 04 / REGENERATE

    Make the next version
