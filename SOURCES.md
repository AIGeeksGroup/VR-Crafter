## Related work: ART-DECO

Qimin Chen, Yuezhi Yang, Wang Yifan, Vladimir G. Kim, Siddhartha Chaudhuri, Hao Zhang, and Zhiqin Chen. **ART-DECO: Arbitrary Text Guidance for 3D Detailizer Construction.** SIGGRAPH Asia 2025.

- Project and original context: https://qiminchen.github.io/artdeco/
- Video: https://qiminchen.github.io/artdeco/demo.mp4
- Shape and style figure: https://qiminchen.github.io/artdeco/representative_image.jpg
- Results: https://qiminchen.github.io/artdeco/result_1.png
- Procedural editing: https://qiminchen.github.io/artdeco/procedural_editing.png

These are related-work examples, credited on the page, not VR-Crafter results. The ART-DECO poster is a frame extracted from its original video. Original media files are stored without modification.
